We are looking for a Senior Embedded Systems Engineer with 6+ years of experience in
hardware and firmware development. The role is based in our Bangalore lab and requires
full-time on-site presence due to the hands-on nature of the work.

Required skills include proficiency in C and C++ for embedded systems, FPGA programming
using VHDL or Verilog, and PCB design with Altium Designer. Experience with JTAG
debugging and oscilloscope-based signal analysis is required. Familiarity with ARM
Cortex-M microcontrollers and FreeRTOS is essential.

Experience with MATLAB and Simulink for hardware-in-the-loop simulation is preferred.
Knowledge of LabVIEW is a plus.

Responsibilities include designing and verifying digital hardware subsystems, writing
low-level firmware for real-time control applications, debugging hardware-software
interfaces using oscilloscopes and logic analyzers, collaborating with mechanical and
RF teams, and owning the bring-up of new prototype boards from schematic review
through validation.

The ideal candidate should be comfortable with ambiguous hardware problems and rapid
prototyping cycles. Strong communication skills are important for cross-disciplinary
collaboration.
