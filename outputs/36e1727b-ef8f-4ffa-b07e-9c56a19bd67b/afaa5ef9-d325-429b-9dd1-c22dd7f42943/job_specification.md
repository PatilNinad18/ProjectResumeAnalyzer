## 4. Evidence & Evaluation Rules

### Understanding of cloud platforms (AWS / Azure / GCP)
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Cloud platforms provide scalable infrastructure for AI workloads via IaaS/PaaS services.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Deployed production ML models on AWS SageMaker or Azure ML; Managed cloud cost optimization for AI pipelines
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Listed cloud services without deployment context; Mentioned cloud in job descriptions without hands-on experience
> - **Screening Question:** Have you deployed at least one production AI model using AWS, Azure, or GCP?

### Knowledge of deployment, Docker, and basic DevOps practices
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Docker containers package applications with dependencies for consistent deployment across environments.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Built and containerized AI models using Dockerfiles; Integrated CI/CD pipelines for model deployments
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Used Docker in academic projects without production impact; Mentioned containerization without deployment metrics
> - **Screening Question:** Can you describe a time you containerized an AI model and deployed it to production?

### Ability to quickly prototype AI-driven products
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Rapid AI prototyping involves creating testable models with minimal code for fast iteration.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Built 3+ AI prototypes in <2 weeks using frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow; Used MLOps tools to validate prototypes before production
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Prototyped models in Jupyter notebooks without deployment; Mentioned 'quick prototyping' without metrics or ownership
> - **Screening Question:** Give an example of an AI prototype you shipped within 1 week of concept.

### What We Look For
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** AI Native Engineers design systems from scratch using AI/ML models as core components, not just add-ons.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Built production ML pipelines with end-to-end model deployment; Deployed custom AI models via TensorFlow Serving or similar
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Mentioned 'AI' in projects without technical implementation details; Used generic terms like 'intelligent systems' without concrete examples
> - **Screening Question:** Describe a production AI system you built where models directly impacted business metrics.

### Strong problem-solving mindset
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Problem-solving in engineering involves breaking complex issues into testable components with data-driven validation.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Debugged production systems using A/B testing and root cause analysis; Reduced latency by 30%+ through systematic optimization
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Said 'I solve problems' without specific examples or metrics; Used vague terms like 'thinking outside the box' without context
> - **Screening Question:** Walk me through how you diagnosed a production issue that impacted user experience.

### Ability to work in fast-paced startup environment
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Startup environments demand rapid iteration, minimal viable product delivery, and adaptability to changing priorities.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Launched features in <2 weeks with clear user metrics; Scaled systems from 1k to 100k users during early growth phase
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Worked in 'fast-paced' environments without specific examples; Mentioned 'startup culture' without ownership of delivery timelines
> - **Screening Question:** How did you adapt your technical approach when a key business goal changed mid-sprint?

### Passion for AI-first product building
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Building products where AI is the core driver, not an add-on feature.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Deployed AI models directly in production without manual intervention; Reduced user tasks by 30%+ through AI features in live products
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Mentioned AI in a portfolio but no metrics or ownership; Used AI as a feature in a prototype without real-world impact
> - **Screening Question:** Can you share an example where your AI product reduced user effort by 20%+ in production?

### Ownership-driven and self-motivated attitude
> **[AI Guidance — Evaluator Aid]**
> - **Concept Summary:** Proactively driving work without waiting for direction, with clear outcomes.
> - **Strong Candidate Signals:** Launched features independently with measurable business impact; Owned end-to-end delivery of projects from ideation to production
> - **Weak / Ambiguous Signals:** Volunteered for tasks without ownership or metrics; Completed tasks under supervision without initiative
> - **Screening Question:** Tell me about a time you took initiative to solve a problem without being asked.
